Real tools' work, timed three ways

Each workload is something a real AnkiConnect tool does, taken from its code. It runs against the same Anki collection through AnkiConnect and through Tsunagi's AnkiConnect shim, sending exactly what the tool sends, and through the Tsunagi API, written the way an app would use it.

Run

What was measured

A real Japanese mining collection. In a run, all three APIs ran in the same conditions; between runs they can differ, so compare APIs within a run.

Every workload

Each dot is one timed run; the bar is the middle one. Each workload has its own scale, from zero to its slowest run. Press Play to race the three: in Time, each bar fills at the measured pace; in Requests, a ball goes from the tool to Anki and back once per request. Show each request splits each bar into its requests: in Time, the middle run's requests where they happened, the gaps being the tool's own time between them; in Requests, one segment per request. Hover one for what it was.
